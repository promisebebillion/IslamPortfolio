## Visual direction

Palette: cinema black #101212, warm white #f2f2e9, slate #8b9390, electric chartreuse #d7ff3f, charcoal #232827.

Typography: expressive Instrument Serif for the editorial statements; Manrope for navigation, descriptions and the oversized personal wordmark. Small technical figures refer to actual video durations and aspect ratios.

Composition: the hero pairs a large editorial statement with a portrait triptych taken from the client's actual work. A compact reel is the only automatic video download. A portrait-native work gallery follows, with company/category filters and a dedicated before/after section. Light biography section gives reading contrast. Contact section remains direct and useful.

Signature: the editing cut is the graphic motif (offset frames, cut mark, linear progress), rather than adding unrelated 3D decoration. Motion responds to user actions, with a reduced-motion option.

## Media strategy

Keep every source file untouched. Generate posters, a short silent hero reel, and web-optimized MP4s with metadata at the front. The gallery renders images only; full videos are mounted after opening a project. The player starts with a lighter rendition and offers HD. Maintain portrait/landscape aspect ratios and use contain in playback. Before/after sources are explicitly labeled and synchronised when compared side by side.

Reference: https://web.dev/learn/performance/video-performance and https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.html

HTTP hosting must support Range requests. Streaming HLS can be added through dedicated video hosting if the deployed audience requires adaptive bitrate; a CDN-served fast-start MP4 with demand loading is sufficient for this short-form collection.
